ROKSTONE BAR / CONCEPT PRESENTATION
Hospitality Design Concept

ROK
STONE

A signature suspended bar feature bringing together Jamaican rum, tropical planting, warm architectural lighting and a contemporary hospitality language.

01 / Overall Concept

A focal point above the bar.

The new intervention is concentrated overhead, allowing the existing bar, columns, stools, flooring and surrounding architecture to remain visually intact while giving ROKstone a stronger, purpose-built identity.

02 / What Was Added

One suspended system.
Six deliberate moves.

01
Two-Tier Liquor Display

Two distinct overhead shelf levels create depth, hierarchy and room for a curated physical rum presentation.

02
Physical Bottle Presentation

Liquor remains the visual priority, with bottles treated as real dimensional objects rather than graphic panels.

03
Tropical Greenery

Ferns and trailing plants soften the structure and introduce movement without overpowering the bottles.

04
Warm Integrated Lighting

Architectural lighting defines the shelves and selected bottle moments, supporting the shift from day into evening.

05
Dark Structural Frame

A restrained matte-black framework gives the feature a contemporary, fabricated quality and keeps it visually light.

06
Heritage Storytelling

A central graphic moment gives the installation a place to reference Kingston, Jamaica and the broader rum story without turning the feature into a single-brand display.

03 / Rum + Greenery

Liquor remains the hero.

The planting is intentionally secondary. Its role is to soften the metalwork, add a lush tropical layer and help the suspended installation feel integrated into the hotel environment while the rum collection stays immediately legible.

04 / Lighting

Day to evening.

Warm integrated light gives the display presence after sunset, adds hierarchy to premium bottle moments and complements the existing hospitality finishes without reading as neon or theatrical lighting.

05 / Architectural Integration

Designed around what is already there.

Columns

Existing columns remain unchanged; the feature works around them.

Bar Geometry

The existing counter and service layout remain visually intact.

Sightlines

The suspended composition frames the space without closing off the open views.

Operation

The concept is intended to preserve practical bar use while adding a strong visual identity overhead.

06 / Design Intent

A contemporary rum showcase that feels purpose-built for ROKstone — not simply a liquor shelf suspended above the bar.
