Music ArtworkCover Design

Mavado — Big Money

Cover artwork built around a bold artist-led composition with the title functioning as both typography and image.

Design choices

The oversized serif title creates immediate recognition and is filled with a money texture to reinforce the track concept without adding another competing graphic element. The artist overlaps the lettering to create depth, while floating currency and the dark night setting extend the theme. Muted green highlights tie the portrait, type and money imagery together.

Event Design2018

Caliente — Cinco de Mayo

High-energy event flyer created to communicate a themed nightlife event while carrying a large amount of practical information.

Design choices

The visual language deliberately leans into a loud hand-illustrated festival aesthetic: layered outlines, expressive lettering, tequila imagery and repeating Mexican-inspired motifs. Orange establishes the atmosphere while red, green, black and white create contrast. Event details are grouped into distinct illustrated blocks so the dense information remains scannable.

Campaign GraphicPhotography Promotion2021

Jik-Reuben — LinkedIn Headshots

Promotional graphic for a professional-headshot offer aimed at young professionals, entrepreneurs and business owners.

Design choices

The upper half uses polished portraiture and neutral corporate tones to establish credibility immediately. A strong red information field creates a clear break between imagery and offer details, while yellow is reserved for the promotional hook and supporting emphasis. Angular image panels and diagonal line accents add energy without undermining the professional positioning.

Campaign GraphicStudent Promotion2021

Jik-Reuben — Student One Day Special

Student-focused professional portrait promotion designed to feel accessible and youthful while retaining the credibility of a photography brand.

Design choices

The notebook-paper background, tape details and handwritten typography create an intentionally informal student visual language. Bright yellow, cyan, red and blue build urgency and distinguish the offer from more conventional corporate photography advertising. The portrait remains dominant so the final piece still sells the quality of the photography rather than only the discount.
