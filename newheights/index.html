About New Heights

Building more than structures.

New Heights Construction & Development Ltd. and R.D.Y. Construction Co. Ltd. approach construction with an understanding of its environmental, social and economic impact. Integrity, teamwork and commitment guide the company’s work.

Founder & CEO Devon Young formed the company in 2005. The business grew from plumbing, roof repair and civil engineering into broader building construction and development.
