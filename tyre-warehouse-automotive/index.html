The proposition01 / 04

From service
centre to
showroom.

Tyre Warehouse Automotive is envisioning the transformation of its established Old Hope Road location into a dedicated vehicle display and retail destination.

Existing service bays give way to an expansive, glazed showroom that puts the vehicle range at the forefront—creating a cohesive environment for product presentation, customer engagement and brand visibility.

The architectural approach pairs a strong commercial presence with an approachable, contemporary experience.

Transformation of an established automotive location
Site context02 / 04

A familiar location. A new purpose.

Built on an existing automotive presence.

The proposal preserves the value of the site's recognizable roadside position while giving it a new commercial role: a dedicated setting for showcasing vehicles and welcoming prospective customers.

Its prominent frontage offers a clear opportunity for a distinctive and memorable retail identity.
