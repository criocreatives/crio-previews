Face Marketing · Billboard Concept

Turning the billboard into a landmark.

A sculptural architectural treatment designed to make a conventional digital billboard feel distinctive before any advertising content is even displayed.

Design Direction

A controlled weave, not a conventional frame.

The concept separates the billboard's functional support from its visual identity. A solid black support wall creates a clean architectural backdrop, while a deliberate sequence of magenta linear members crosses the surface around the display. The screen remains the focal point; the surrounding structure gives it presence.

The geometry is intentionally more ordered than a random lattice. Repeated opposing diagonals create rhythm and movement while keeping the overall installation practical to fabricate, mount and maintain.

01

16:9 Display

The digital screen uses a familiar 16:9 proportion, keeping advertising content easy to produce and the overall composition compact.

02

Solid Black Support Wall

A dark backing plane visually anchors the installation, hides conventional support elements and gives the illuminated members maximum contrast.

03

Organized Linear Rhythm

The rods follow a repeatable crossing logic rather than appearing haphazard. The pattern frames the display without simply drawing another rectangle around it.

04

Selective Integrated Light

Magenta/purple members provide identity during the day; integrated linear illumination activates selected lines at night for a stronger roadside presence.

Core Idea
The structure does not compete with the advertisement. It makes the place where the advertisement lives memorable.
